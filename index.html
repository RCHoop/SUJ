Why we stand up

Jesus gave us an assignment: to build His Kingdom, not a denomination's roster. Being saved is the first step. The rest is walking with Jesus and, as part of His army, standing up for God's principles in a world that has pushed Him out of the public square, the board room, our schools and even the family table.

We have been quiet long enough. Standing Up For Jesus is a multi-denominational movement of ordinary believers who carry their faith, with love and courage, into every part of daily life.

The Public Square: speak at public meetings

School boards, planning and zoning boards, city councils and county boards often set aside time for the public to speak. In Arizona it is called the "call to the public." Here is a simple way to use it.

  1. Find the meeting. Look up the agenda and the board's own rules for public comment. Rules differ by state and by board.
  2. Sign up to speak. Most boards use a card or sign-in sheet before the meeting starts.
  3. Prepare three minutes. Tell them what you will tell them, tell them, then tell them what you told them. Speak from a Jesus point of view, with respect.
  4. Bring others. Each speaker gets their own time, so several voices can carry one message.
  5. Follow up. Email the officials and their staff, local media and your church leaders.

General information, not legal advice. Whether a board must offer public comment, and for how long, depends on your state's law and the board's rules.

How we stand in public

School board meetings, the American Legion hall, the garden club: there are countless public places to stand up for Jesus. Ambassadors are not there to be confrontational, obnoxious or strange. With love, we stand for Jesus' principles and the Biblical values that come from them.

"Weird is not holy. It's just weird."Pastor Allen Jackson

We will not stand by quietly while the world insists that rejecting those principles is not only acceptable but required. This is where the spiritual battle becomes personal. As Bob Dylan sang in "Gotta Serve Somebody," no one gets to stay neutral. Everyone serves someone, so each of us has to choose.

Our Ambassadors have chosen: "But as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD." Joshua 24:15 (NKJV)

Government at all levels: hold them to the issues

Learn the issues before your city, county, state and nation. Write to your officials and their staff, and speak to the issues with grace and truth. When it is time to speak in person, use the public meeting steps above.

A Warrior's Proclamation

As a warrior for God, I proclaim:

  • I am the salt of the earth. Matt 5:13
  • I am the light of the world. Matt 5:14
  • Jesus is my Lord; I am a child of God. He has chosen me. John 1:12-13
  • I am a joint heir with Christ. Rom 8:17
  • I am a new creation, reconciled to God. 2 Cor 5:17-18
  • I am God's workmanship, created for His purpose. Eph 2:10
  • I am an Ambassador for Jesus of Nazareth, my Lord and King. 2 Cor 5:20

For God, I will stand my ground. "The Sovereign Lord is my strength." Hab 3:19

The Warrior's Prayer. I am a child of Almighty God; Jesus is Lord of my life, and the Holy Spirit is my helper. God Himself has called me for His purposes. I choose God's path with my entire being. May God give me the strength I need to complete the course He has chosen. Amen.
